# Visual Film Asset Generation Prompts (High-Retention Timeline)
**Song:** "मुझे अपना बनाकर छोड़ गए"  
**Aesthetic Style:** Traditional Indian Miniature, Pichwai & Wash Painting on warm antique handmade paper grain (`#efe2d0`).  
**Visual Pacing:** Fast, dynamic transitions (approx. 2–3 seconds per plate / 3–5 shots per 10-second window) with deliberate negative space left on one side for synchronized animated lyric typography.

---

## 🎨 Master Style & Composition Rules for AI Generators
When generating these images, always ensure:
1. **Paper Consistency:** The background texture must look like aged Rajasthani/Vrindavan handmade parchment with subtle ink bleeding and sepia tones.
2. **Text Space (Anchor Area):** Leave the designated quadrant (e.g. Right Half, Upper Third, Left Half) free of heavy details or high-contrast figures so Devnagari lyrics stay readable.
3. **No Embedded Text:** Prompts explicitly forbid the AI from writing words or English/Hindi letters into the artwork.
4. **Color Palette:** Warm terracotta, indigo, saffron, turmeric yellow, soft forest green, and sacred madder red.
